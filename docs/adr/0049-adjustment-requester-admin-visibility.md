# Requester and admin visibility for Adjustment requests

Pending and rejected Adjustment requests are visible to the requester and Group owners/admins only. Other members do not see another member's pending or rejected correction request. Once approved, the immutable Adjustment and its allocation deltas become shared Group financial history. A rejected request remains audit history for the requester and administrators but has no financial effect.

The alternatives — exposing every request to all active members or hiding requests from their requester — either reveal private correction context unnecessarily or prevent the person who submitted the request from understanding its status. Requester/admin-only visibility preserves accountability and least privilege while keeping approved financial outcomes transparent.

The trade-off is that ordinary members cannot inspect pending correction activity before it affects the ledger. The approved result and its reason become visible when the correction is financially effective.
